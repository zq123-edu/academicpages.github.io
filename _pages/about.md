## About Me

I am Qi Zhang, an Assistant Professor at City University of Macau. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Macau under the supervision of [Prof. Bob Zhang](https://www.fst.um.edu.mo/people/bobzhang/).

## Academic Position

- Assistant Professor, City University of Macau, present

## Research Interests

My research interests include:

- Machine learning
- Smart healthcare
- Pattern recognition
- Image processing
- Artificial intelligence applications

I am looking for highly motivated postgraduate and undergraduate students interested in machine learning, smart healthcare, pattern recognition, image processing, and artificial intelligence applications. Interested students are welcome to contact me by email.
